Mandatory Running Staff Protocols • HWH Division

1. SPAD Prevention Measures
  • Strictly call out signals loud and clear between LP & ALP with hand gestures.
  • Begin controlled braking at least 1000m before a Danger (Red) stop signal.
  • Ensure speed is reduced to not more than 15 kmph at 100m before stop signal.
  • No mobile phone usage while on footplate (Strict Zero-Tolerance Policy).
2. Neutral Section Coasting
  • Open DJ / VCB at 500m / 250m Warning Board before Neutral Section.
  • Lower pantograph immediately if instructed due to OHE defect or emergency.
  • Close DJ / VCB only after passing the neutral section indicator board.
  • Never stall inside a Neutral Section under any circumstances.
3. Platform Entry Speed Checking
  • Observe maximum 15 kmph / 30 kmph turnout speed entering platform lines.
  • Check brake feel test at first opportunity after clearing starting yard.
  • Continuous speed monitoring with V-cutoff adherence on SPM recorder.
  • Stop with gentle brake release before complete halt to avoid jerk.
4. Cattle Run Over (CRO) & Derailment Protection
  • Apply emergency brake and switch ON Flasher Light immediately.
  • Inform Section Controller (TLC/PCNL) and adjacent station on VHF walkie-talkie.
  • Examine brake pipe continuity and ensure adjacent line is not fouled.
  • Record all details in loco log book before restarting train.

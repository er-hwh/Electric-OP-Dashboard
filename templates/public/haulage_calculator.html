Scientific Formulation & Indian Railways Standards
1. Grade & Curvature Compensation

Equivalent gradient combines direct geometric incline with track curve resistance:

1 / X = (1 / G) + (0.4 × Curve_Degrees / 1000)
Where G = Gradient slope ratio, 0.4 kg/t per degree of curve resistance.
2. Wet Rail Adhesion Factor (TFC-77)

Per Railway Board TFC-77 standard, nominal adhesion under wet/monsoon conditions undergoes a 20% reduction:

TE_wet = TE_dry × 0.80  •  μ_wet = 0.80 × μ_dry
3. Rolling Stock Resistance (RDSO)

Loaded BOXN Wagons resistance polynomial (kg/tonne):

R_T = 0.64387 + (0.010472 × V) + (0.00007323 × V²)
4. Locomotive Starting Resistance

Starting specific resistance at 0 km/h:

R_L(start) = 6.0 kg/tonne  •  R_T(start) = 4.0 kg/t (Freight) / 4.5 kg/t (ICF)

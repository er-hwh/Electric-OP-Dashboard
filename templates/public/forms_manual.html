Standardised Operating Forms & Authorities (Appendix 'D')

Official Operating Forms used in Train Working under Eastern Railway General & Subsidiary Rules

Back to E-Learning
S.No. Form No. Description of Operating Form Font / Paper Colour Operational Context & Rule Authority
1 T/409 Caution Order Green Font / Green Paper Issued to Loco Pilot & Guard detailing permanent/temporary speed restrictions, locations, and reasons (GR 4.09).
2 T/A 409 'NIL' Caution Order Green Font Issued by Notice Station when there are no special speed restrictions in force on the run (SR 4.09).
3 T/B 409 Reminder Caution Order Green Font Issued by rear station to remind crew of caution order before entering affected block section.
4 T/369-3b Authority to Pass Signals in 'ON' or Defective Position Blue Font Handed over to Loco Pilot at the foot of defective signal after points setting and clamping is confirmed (GR 3.69 / 3.70).
5 T/369(1) Advance Authority to Pass Defective Signals Blue Font Issued at station in rear to advise Loco Pilot in advance regarding defective signal ahead.
6 T/806 Shunting Order Blue Font Authorizes shunting past starter or into sidings when movements foul running lines (GR 5.14).
7 T/509 Authority to Receive a Train on an Obstructed Line Blue Font Issued when train must be received on a line already occupied (GR 5.09).
8 T/511 Authority to Start from a Non-Signalled Line Blue Font Starting order when train starts from a line where no starter signal is provided (GR 5.11).
9 T/A 602 Authority to Proceed for Relief Engine into Occupied Block Section Red Font Given to assisting locomotive to proceed without line clear into section with disabled train (GR 6.05).
10 T/D 602 Authority for Temporary Single Line Working on Double Line Section Red Font Issued to Loco Pilot for wrong line operation during track obstruction (SR 6.02).
11 T/C 1425 Paper Line Clear Ticket (UP) Blue Font (Up Arrow) Physical authority to proceed when electrical block instrument fails on UP direction (GR 14.25).
12 T/D 1425 Paper Line Clear Ticket (DOWN) Blue Font (Down Arrow) Physical authority to proceed when electrical block instrument fails on DOWN direction (GR 14.25).
13 T/A 912 Authority to Pass Automatic/Semi-Automatic Stop Signals at 'ON' Blue Font Used in Automatic Block territory during prolonged signal failure (GR 9.12 / App. E).
14 T/B 912 Authority to Proceed Without Line Clear on Automatic Block Territory Red Font Emergency movement during total communication failure in automatic section.

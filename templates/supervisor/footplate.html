    G&SR 3.07, 3.08

    STANDARD REMARKS: Speed controlled as prescribed Speed not controlled in time Speed above prescribed limit Not prepared to stop (Critical)

    Whistling at LC Gates, Curves & W/L Boards
    G&SR 4.50
    5 ★ Exemplary

    Intermittent whistling from W/L board up to gates; continuous whistling at blind curves & unmanned crossings.

    STANDARD REMARKS: Whistle given correctly Whistle given late Duration inadequate Whistle missed at curve (Critical) Whistle missed at LC gate (Critical)

    Brake Feel (BFT) & Brake Power (BPT) Test
    Joint Safety Procedure
    5 ★ Exemplary

    STANDARD REMARKS: Both tests done as prescribed BFT at incorrect location BPT at incorrect location Test result not assessed BFT not done (Critical) BPT not done (Critical)
